**Short description**
Create editable social quote cards in a guided inline editor.

**Long description**
Quote Card Builder turns a quote you choose into an editable social card. Select a visible
attribution, a neutral or custom color palette, and an Editorial, Poster, or Frame direction, then
refine line breaks, formatting, composition, and motif in the inline editor. Preview and generation
use the same canonical renderer. The app prepares a PNG that opens in a browser tab so you can save
it from the browser. It does not publish your card or retain generated files on its application
server.

## Accuracy notes for reviewers

- The app creates one quote card at a time.
- It does not publish to social networks or save a library of cards.
- It does not generate a photographic image or use a second image-generation model.
- The public MCP service is stateless at the application layer.
- The local skill contains a broader Visual Review Studio workflow; the remote inline app is the
  deliberately smaller marketplace surface described above.
